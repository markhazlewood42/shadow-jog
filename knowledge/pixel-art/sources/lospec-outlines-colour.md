# Lospec: Pixel Art Outlines, Part 2, Using Color

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://lospec.com/articles/pixel-art-outlines-part-2-using-color

- **Author:** Lospec
- **Kind:** Primary-ish (a tutorial article)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full. Part 1 I did not open.

## What it covers

Coloured outlines, selective outlining and internal lines.

## Key points, in my words

- Rule: an outline should always increase contrast and never decrease it.
- A darker shade of the object works on light backgrounds; selective outlining varies darkness with the sprite's own shading, but varied backgrounds can force black.
- Internal lines can be lighter, fade toward connections and break to hint at sub-pixel detail; where colours meet, usually use the colour of the nearer part.

## Best for

The contrast rule and the internal-line guidance.

## Caveats

Selout here means a lighter outline in lit areas, which is one of three uses of the word.
