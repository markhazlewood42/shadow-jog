## What it covers

What sub-pixel animation is and how to do it.

## Key points, in my words

- A sprite cannot sit half a pixel from a grid line, so slow motion looks jerky.
- The technique adds and removes pixels at moving edges across frames so the shape appears to slide.
- Works better on larger sprites and simple regions; avoid smearing detailed areas.
