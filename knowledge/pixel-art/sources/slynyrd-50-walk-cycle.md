# Slynyrd (Raymond Schlitter): Pixelblog 50, Human Walk Cycle

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2024/5/24/pixelblog-50-human-walk-cycle

- **Author:** Raymond Schlitter (Slynyrd)
- **Kind:** Primary (a working game artist's tutorial series)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

A reference human walk cycle reduced from 72 frames at 60 fps to 8, 6 and 4 frames.

## Key points, in my words

- Four positions: contact, down, passing, swing.
- Eight frames is "the optimum"; four captures the motion and six is a compromise.
- Head bob follows a triangular wave, not a sine; arm swing and detail differ by character personality.
- Keep fold lines and details minimal to control the cost of extra frames.

## Best for

The structure of a walk and frame trade-offs.

## Caveats

Realistic proportions at a large size; small chibi sprites simplify heavily.
