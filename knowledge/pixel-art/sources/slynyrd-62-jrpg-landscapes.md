# Slynyrd (Raymond Schlitter): Pixelblog 62, Landscape Backgrounds

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2026/5/27/pixelblog-62-landscape-backgrounds

- **Author:** Raymond Schlitter (Slynyrd)
- **Kind:** Primary (a working game artist's tutorial series)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

JRPG battle backgrounds at 192 by 144 in 15 colours: valley, desert and forest, plus monster designs.

## Key points, in my words

- Build depth from stacked horizontal colour bands; atmospheric perspective lowers saturation, raises lightness and shifts hue toward the sky colour with distance.
- Texture shrinks with distance: thick blades up close, one or two pixel marks beyond, then none.
- Reuse colours across several jobs to stay near 15 colours.
- Monsters from real animals with a fantasy twist; scale to 4x before applying CRT effects.

## Best for

The best match for JRPG battle backdrops.

## Caveats

Illustrative at one resolution; dated 2026.
