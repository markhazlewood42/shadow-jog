## What it covers

Reading bars at a glance, segmented bars, and nine-slice panels.

## Key points, in my words

- A bar is read by how full it is: contrast between fill and track, a dark frame, and segmenting for countability.
- Nine-slice: four fixed corners, four edges stretching one way, a centre stretching both ways; use one base unit.
