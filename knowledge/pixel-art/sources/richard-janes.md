# Richard Janes: Introduction to Pixel Art

- **Author:** Richard Janes

## What it covers

Four basics (line art, colours, dithering, shading with anti-aliasing) and a worked ceramic-pot sprite.

## Key points, in my words

- Five straight line types are clean because their runs are consistent; avoid irregular lines and fix curves by hand.
- Colour: use warm colours for light and cool for shadow, keep enough contrast between shades, and rarely use more than five shades of one colour.
- Dithering in three stages (50:50 checker, then 25:75 and 75:25 dots); sparingly today but still useful.
- Pillow shading is named as a major crime and a poor stand-in for anti-aliasing.
- Pot exercise: flat fill, first shade, highlight and next shade, last shade, outline replaced by a dark tone.

## Best for

A compact old-school view and a good first exercise (the pot).
