# Boris the Brave: Tileset Roundup

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.boristhebrave.com/2013/07/14/tileset-roundup/

- **Author:** Boris the Brave
- **Kind:** Primary (a developer's survey)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

Four autotile approaches: marching squares, the blob, sub-blob (RPG Maker) and micro-blob.

## Key points, in my words

- Marching squares: 16 tiles, the index is TL + 2 TR + 4 BL + 8 BR, popular but visibly repetitive.
- Blob: 47 tiles plus an empty one, with a lookup from neighbour patterns; variety only if the artist works for it.
- Sub-blob: about 20 quarter-tile pieces assembled into many combinations; micro-blob: 13 pieces, rarely used.

## Best for

Choosing an autotile system by artist workload.

## Caveats

Dated (2013) but the systems have not changed.
