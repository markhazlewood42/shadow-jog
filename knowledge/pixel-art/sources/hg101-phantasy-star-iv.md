# Hardcore Gaming 101: Phantasy Star IV

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.hardcoregaming101.net/phantasy-star-iv/

- **Author:** Hardcore Gaming 101 (review)
- **Kind:** Secondary (game journalism)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read; the review says little about specific sprites or palettes.

## What it covers

The game's presentation: panel storytelling, the behind-the-back battle view and its pacing, character art and cartridge size.

## Key points, in my words

- Manga-style panels gradually fill the screen during key scenes, made by the same artists as the game, as a lower-memory counterpart to CD-based cutscenes; the game was first planned for a CD add-on.
- Battles return to the Phantasy Star II behind-the-back view with elaborate animation on both sides, sped up from earlier games.
- Detailed portraits for the main cast; the cartridge held four times Phantasy Star II's capacity.

## Best for

Understanding what the reference game is doing, not how.
