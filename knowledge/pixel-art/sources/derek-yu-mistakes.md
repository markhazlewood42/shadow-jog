# Derek Yu: Pixel Art, Common Mistakes

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.derekyu.com/makegames/pixelart2.html

- **Author:** Derek Yu
- **Kind:** Primary
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full. Last updated 28 January 2020.

## What it covers

A before-and-after rework of a naive picture to show four faults: too many similar colours, naive colouring, pillow shading and stiff "cardboard" designs.

## Key points, in my words

- Each colour should have its own identity so it does as much work as possible; blending colours is fine if done on purpose, such as muting a background.
- Naive colouring assigns colours by label; fix it with existing palettes, observation and experiment with brightness and saturation.
- Pillow shading rarely occurs in nature; think in forms with volume (tree canopies as clustered spheres, a nest as a bowl).
- The chunky pixels rule: avoid one-pixel-thick parts, since they cannot be shaded into forms.
- Exaggerate characteristic features and think beyond the grid. The article ends by treating improvement as a personal journey with no endpoint.

## Best for

Teaching the "why" behind four faults with matched images.

## Caveats

The images carry much of the lesson and a text reader loses them.
