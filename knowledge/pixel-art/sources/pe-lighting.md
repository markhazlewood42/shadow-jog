## What it covers

A guided build of a 48 by 32 lit room: place the light, brighten the source, add dim shells, let the room catch the light, and cast a shadow.

## Key points, in my words

- A glowing object has three jobs: the source, the glow (optional, stylistic) and the illumination of nearby surfaces.
- Few pixels should carry the brightest value; surface patches must stay dimmer than the core.
- A cast shadow is a separate shape from the dark side of the object and should run away from the source and touch the object.
- Warm and cool versions change only the light colours and not the shadow direction.
