Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://saint11.art/blog/pixel-art-tutorials/

- **Author:** Pedro Medeiros (saint11)
- **Kind:** Primary (index only)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** I could read only the index of titles; each tutorial is a 512 by 512 animated image and carries no text for a fetcher.

## What it covers

Dozens of free image tutorials on fundamentals, shading, outlines, silhouette, sub-pixel animation, walk and run cycles, attacks, fire, water, ice, rock, metal, wood, fabric, vegetation, effects, UI nine-slice, portraits and more.

## Key points, in my words

- The titles show what a full curriculum covers: Fundamentals, Shading, Outlines, Silhouette, Subpixel, Squash, Easings, Tiles, Parallax, Portrait, UI-9-Slice, Cuteness and material studies.
- Free to read; a zip is sold to support the author.
