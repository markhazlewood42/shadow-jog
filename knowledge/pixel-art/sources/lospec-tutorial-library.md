# Lospec: Pixel Art Tutorials (index and tag pages)

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://lospec.com/pixel-art-tutorials

- **Author:** Lospec community
- **Kind:** Index
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read the index and three tag pages (animation, color, dithering). The author-filter page for AdamCYounis returned no entries.

## What it covers

A searchable list of hundreds of tutorials in text, video, image and book form, by topic, author and medium.

## Key points, in my words

- Lists Michael Azzi's paid book "Pixel Logic", Pedro Medeiros's short animation lessons, Slynyrd, Luke Sadface, Cure's tutorial and a 2007 Derek Yu tutorial.
- Tag pages for dithering and colour are short, which shows how few strong written lessons exist on those topics.

## Best for

Finding further reading.

## Caveats

An index, so every claim about a tutorial's content comes from the tutorial itself.
