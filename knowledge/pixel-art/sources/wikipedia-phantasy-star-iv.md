## What it covers

Release, team, manga-style panels, combination skills and reception.

## Key points, in my words

- Released in Japan on 17 December 1993 (North America in 1995) for the Sega Genesis; directed by Rieko Kodama, Toru Yoshida and Kiyoshi Takeuchi.
- New manga-style panels and combination "macros"; contemporary reviews of the graphics were split, retrospective ones favourable.
