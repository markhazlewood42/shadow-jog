## What it covers

A worked cylinder exercise in two colours across four stages, a density table, six ways to keep dithering readable, and adaptations for sky, stone and polished metal.

## Key points, in my words

- Stage one is a flat silhouette, then a hard shadow split, then a checkerboard bridge, then 75, 50 and 25 percent coverage.
- Coverage is the count of light pixels over the total; it says which colour a patch leans toward and is not a brightness measurement.
- Stone: keep solid lit and shadow faces and make the border irregular. Metal: keep the narrow bright reflection solid, because dither on top reads as roughness.
- Points to Derek Yu's tutorial for a gradient, banded and dithered comparison.

## Best for

A short hands-on exercise that can be assigned as is.

## Caveats

Written for the editor's audience, with swatches and not finished art.
