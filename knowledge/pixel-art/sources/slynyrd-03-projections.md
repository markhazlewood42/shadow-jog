# Slynyrd (Raymond Schlitter): Pixelblog 3, Graphical Projections, Part 1

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2018/3/14/pixelblog-3-graphical-projections-1

- **Author:** Raymond Schlitter (Slynyrd)
- **Kind:** Primary (a working game artist's tutorial series)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

The 3/4 top-down and 3/4 side projections used for buildings and scenes.

## Key points, in my words

- "Uniformity takes priority over realism": every element in a scene should obey the same projection rules and angles.
- Depth comes mostly from strong lighting on simple geometry, with ledges that catch light; palettes need clear contrast.
- Outline before colouring complex structures, or block in colour if you know your scheme.

## Best for

Understanding why games bend perspective.

## Caveats

Architecture-focused, but the author says it carries over to characters and creatures.
