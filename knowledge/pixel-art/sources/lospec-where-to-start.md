## What it covers

Tools, tutorials, a four-part improvement loop, communities and related art skills.

## Key points, in my words

- Try the browser editor before installing; use a ready-made palette to avoid decision paralysis.
- Recommended starters: Derek Yu's sprite tutorial, Cure's overview and the paid "Pixel Logic".
- Improvement loop: practise, learn from tutorials, seek critique, study good work.
- Communities: Twitter hashtags, r/PixelArt, Pixel Joint (est. 2004), Pixelation.
- Supplement with Draw a Box, CTRL+Paint and animation-principles series.
