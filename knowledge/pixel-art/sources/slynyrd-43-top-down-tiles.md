## What it covers

Seamless base textures, variants, layered tiles, walls, sand, water and the pain of multiple connections.

## Key points, in my words

- A base texture must connect on all four sides while hiding the tile pattern; use a tool that shows tiling live.
- Layering transparent texture tiles avoids baking every combination; edge pieces come from the base.
- Drop shadows on walls get one consistent length; sand uses long angled S clusters; water animates with two layouts plus a half-opacity in-between.
- The first draft took a day and balancing took over a week.

## Best for

Honest, practical tile workflow.

## Caveats

Dependent on tile-mode tools.
