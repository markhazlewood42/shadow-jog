- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

Building punch and sword attacks: stance, smear frames, hold frames, overshoot, range.

## Key points, in my words

- Smears follow the arc of the swing, are usually a single frame and should be few and fast.
- Holding a frame amplifies impact; overshoot suggests momentum; long holds read as awkward.
- Anticipation can read as input lag and long recovery as sluggishness, so snappy beats lavish in playable games.
- Exaggerate limbs at full extension for energy.

## Best for

Smear frames, holds and the responsiveness trade-off.

## Caveats

Written for real-time action games; a turn-based game has more freedom.
