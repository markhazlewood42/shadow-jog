## What it covers

Definition, history from the 1970s to the 2020s, techniques (dithering, anti-aliasing, sub-pixelling), clusters, categories and communities.

## Key points, in my words

- The term appeared in print in 1982 (Xerox PARC); deliberate pixel placement dates from about 1972.
- Timeline: arcades, Deluxe Paint (1985), the demoscene, 1990s classics, hobbyist communities (Pixelation, Pixel Joint), the 2010s indie revival.
- Notes on isometric dimetric projection, Game Boy resolution (160 by 144 and 4 colours) and the use of lossless formats.
