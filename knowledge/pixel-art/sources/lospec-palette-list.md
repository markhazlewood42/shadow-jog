# Lospec: Palette List (and the Resurrect 64 entry)

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://lospec.com/palette-list

- **Author:** Lospec community; Resurrect 64 by Kerrie Lake
- **Kind:** Reference
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read the list page and the Resurrect 64 page.

## What it covers

A database of thousands of palettes filterable by colour count and tag, covering hardware palettes and artist-made ones.

## Key points, in my words

- The list showed 4,499 palettes when I read it, filterable by exact, minimum or maximum colour count.
- The Resurrect 64 entry (64 colours, Kerrie Lake) describes ramps arranged next to each other for easy choosing, with five blues and five blue-greens, warm browns and purples; downloads in several formats.

## Best for

Picking a ready-made palette and loading it into an editor.

## Caveats

Popularity is not quality; test any palette on your own art.
