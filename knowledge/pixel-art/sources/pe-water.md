# Pixel-Editor.com: Rendering 8-Bit Pixel Water

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.pixel-editor.com/articles/rendering-8-bit-pixel-water

## What it covers

Four small water scenes (pond, river channel, shoreline, moon trail) and a five-pass pond build in an 80 by 56 canvas with up to eight colours.

## Key points, in my words

- Water is explained by cues: where the shore is, which way the surface runs, what it reflects and where light catches it.
- Short horizontal strokes, wider toward the viewer, make a flat surface; stair-step marks aligned with a channel suggest a current.
- Break the reflection of a tree into pieces; use few glints; remove half the ripples and see if it still reads.
- States plainly that the eight-colour limit is an exercise constraint and not a claim about old consoles.

## Best for

A clean way to teach one material from first principles.

## Caveats

Scenes are tiny. Moving water tiles need the Slynyrd tile article as well.
