# Wikipedia: Twelve basic principles of animation

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_basic_principles_of_animation

- **Author:** Wikipedia contributors

## What it covers

The twelve Disney principles (Thomas and Johnston, 1981).

## Key points, in my words

- Squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, straight-ahead versus pose-to-pose, follow-through, slow in and out, arcs, secondary action, timing, exaggeration, solid drawing, appeal.

## Best for

A complete list to map to pixel work.

## Caveats

About hand-drawn film, not sprites.
