## What it covers

Defines pixel art by intention and discusses clusters, software, hardware, scaling, and how to grow as an artist.

## Key points, in my words

- "Intention" is the defining factor; automatic anti-aliasing contradicts the medium.
- A cluster is adjacent pixels of one colour; simple angular clusters give a clean look and many organic ones a natural look.
- Scale by whole-number multiples only, with anti-aliasing off; typical canvases are 128 or 256 pixels scaled 2x to 4x.
- Tool notes on Photoshop, Aseprite and Pixel Studio; ergonomic advice (pen tablet, eye care).
- Study old games, follow tutorials, even trace; join challenges such as Pixel Dailies.
