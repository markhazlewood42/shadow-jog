## What it covers

Idle animations and run cycles, with frame counts and timing.

## Key points, in my words

- Start with simple sprites that have clear colour separation; choose a size you can keep up with.
- Early JRPGs often reused walk frames as idles; keep idles simple.
- Mega Man's run uses about three strong frames; a 3-frame cycle at 160 ms feels different from an 8-frame cycle at 80 ms, so retime when you cut frames.
- More frames do not guarantee better motion; extra in-betweens can dilute strong poses; six frames suits many games.
- One orphan pixel in a loop is obvious.

## Best for

Frame economy and timing.

## Caveats

Run-cycle focused.
