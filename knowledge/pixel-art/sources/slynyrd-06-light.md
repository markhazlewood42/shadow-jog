# Slynyrd (Raymond Schlitter): Pixelblog 6, Light and Shadow

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2018/6/15/pixelblog-6-light-and-shadow

- **Author:** Raymond Schlitter (Slynyrd)
- **Kind:** Primary (a working game artist's tutorial series)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

Light as management of colour and clusters to simulate shadow, highlight and mid-tone, and as a tool for mood.

## Key points, in my words

- Establish the light source first; the author prefers a top-left or top-right direction but frames it as a choice.
- Shadow style carries atmosphere: diffuse for haze, crisp for clear air, long and razor-sharp for space.
- Execution is about getting the contrast ratio and cast shadows right.

## Best for

The mood side of lighting.

## Caveats

Short and conceptual: no tone counts, no hue shifting, no list of mistakes.
