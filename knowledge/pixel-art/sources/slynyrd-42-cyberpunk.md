# Slynyrd (Raymond Schlitter): Pixelblog 42, Cyberpunk Pixel Art

- **Kind:** Primary (a working game artist's tutorial series)
- **How I read it (2026-10-01):** Read in full.

## What it covers

The look of cyberpunk in pixels: palette, neon, lighting, character fashion and modular buildings.

## Key points, in my words

- Keep the environment in drab browns and greys and let lighting and neon bring the colour; warm light at street level, cooler higher up.
- Convincing night lighting and neon are the main ingredients.
- Characters at about 16 by 32; start from a nude template and layer clothing and cybernetics.
- Modular building stories duplicated vertically.

## Best for

Setting-specific advice for Shadow Jog's cyberpunk-fantasy world.
