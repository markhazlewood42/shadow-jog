# Saint11 (Pedro Medeiros): Pixel Art Beginner Articles

Part of the [[projects/shadow-jog/knowledge/pixel-art/README|pixel-art library]]. Link: https://saint11.art/pixel_articles/

## What it covers

A free beginner series built around Aseprite.

## Key points, in my words

- Cluster sketching: start with large gestural blocks (a 2 or 3 pixel brush), refine background to foreground, resolve jaggies last; orphans are acceptable in texture, AA and key details.
- Anti-aliasing: a half-tone counts as half a pixel; match AA length to step length; value matters more than hue; skip AA on straights and 45 degrees; practise at 48 by 48. Banding is fixed by band compression or rotating the gradient.
- Colour: HSV; warm highlights and cool shadows with matching saturation shifts; pair large low-saturation areas with small vivid accents; monochrome, complementary and analogous schemes.
- Animation: straight-ahead is friendly to beginners; durations (a 300 ms contact hold) and squash with volume; recommends the Animator's Survival Kit.

## Best for

Short, practical explanations from a leading practitioner.

## Caveats

Aseprite-specific in places.
