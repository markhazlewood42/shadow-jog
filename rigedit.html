Dev tools › Animation editor

Pick someone and a pose. Drag the orange dot (the hand) where it should go: the elbow bends by itself and the arm can't stretch. Drag the blue dot (the elbow) across the arm to bend it the other way, and the green dot to turn the hand and what it holds. The faint figure behind is the pose before this one. Save when it looks right; the game uses it on its next load.
